Our Story

Built on a simple promise: quality without compromise.

Kefayat Hyper Market began in 2016 with a single, radical idea — that a supermarket could feel as premium as a boutique, while remaining accessible to every family.

What started as a 400 sq.ft. corner store has grown into a national brand serving over 50,000 families weekly. Our obsession with sourcing only the finest products — from local farms to imported gourmet labels — remains unchanged.

Today, we operate across multiple cities with a world-class digital platform designed to bring the same premium experience right to your doorstep.
